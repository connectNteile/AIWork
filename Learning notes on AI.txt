Types of machine learning
-supervised learning: working with label or known data and getting feedback. You learn by comparing your results.
-unsupervised learning: working with unknown label or data. so you set for your result by experimenting , looking for similar partterns so as to make conclusion.
-Reinforcement: learning to cook through taste testing. Try different approach 

AI techniques & Approach
-Natural Language Processing(NLP): it is like understanding food description in different language . it is about teaching the computer to understand human languages.
-Computer vision: is like a chef trained eye. it is a technique used to spot if something is undercooked or overcooked. so computer vision allow AI to see and interpret the visual world recognizing objects, people and activities in images and video.
-Generative AI is like a chef who can invent new recipes after learning from thousands of existing recipes. so it can create a new system tht has never existed.
-LLM: they are like master chefs who have memorized every cooking book ever written. They can also write new recipes. LLM is like GPT four, that have been trained in vast amount of text to generate human like response to prompt.

AI architecture components
-Training Data: is like the cooking school curriculum. it is a collection of examples that the AI learns from. AI systems improves by processing many examples( Training data)
-features: are like the characteristics you notice in ingredients. They are measurable properties of data that algorithms uses to make predictions
-model: A model is like a chef's developed intuition and skill set. it is a result of traning agorithm on data capturing patterns that can be applied in a new situation or reused.
-inference: it s like cooking a meal after you have mastered the techniques. so once an AI model, is trained , it can make predictions on new data without having to be trained.

*** Advanced AI Concepts ***
-Overfitting: is like memorizing a recipe so exactly that you cant adapt to different kitchen. An overfitted A model performs well on training data but performs poorly on new data.
BIAS in AI: is like a chef who has only ever cooked one cuisine / dish.
so if AI is trained primarily on data from a demographic set/group, it may perform poorly on another demography or data set.
-Explainable AI (XAI): is like a chef who can describe exactly why they made each decision. XAI aims to make AI decision making transparent and understandable to humans.
-Transfer learning: is like pastry chef switching to bread baking. it allows AI models trained on one task to be repurposed for a related task.


*** AI Ethics & Considerations ***
-privacy concerns: privacy in AI is like respecting secret family recipes. AI system must handle personal data with appropriate protection and consent.
-Ethical AI is like sustainable cooking practices. it considers the broader implication of AI systems on society, fairness and human.
-AI Safety: AI Safety is like kitchen safety protocols. It involves designing systems with safeguard to prevent unintended harmful consequences.

*** Emerging AI Trends ***
-Federated learning: is like chefs improving recipes without sharing their exact ingredients. it allow AI model to be trained across multiple devices without sharing raw data.
-Edge AI: is like cooking at the table rather than on the distant kitchen. Edge AI processes data locally on devices rather than sending everything in distant server, reducing latency and privacy concerns.
-AI Augmentation: is like having a smart kitchen assistant. it aims to enhance human capacities rather than replace them.
-Quantum Machine Learning:  is like discovering entirely new cooking methods beyond conventional heat or method.
-Quantum computing applies quantum mechanical principle to potentially solve certain problems exponentially faster than classic computer.
